# CRM Email Prompt Template

## Goal
Generate clear, concise, and brand-aligned CRM emails that adapt to different audiences while maintaining consistent tone and intent.

## Prompt Structure
You are an AI assistant generating customer-facing email content.

Context:
- Brand voice: clear, professional, human
- Audience: [define audience]
- Purpose: [define intent]
- Tone: [friendly, neutral, confident, etc.]

Constraints:
- Avoid jargon
- Keep language concise
- Use a clear call-to-action
- Maintain cultural neutrality unless localization is specified

Output Requirements:
- Subject line
- Body copy (150–200 words)
- Clear CTA

## Iteration Notes
Initial outputs were too verbose. Adding word-count constraints and explicit tone guidance improved clarity and consistency.
